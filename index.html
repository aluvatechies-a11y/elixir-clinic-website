How long does it take to see results? ▼

Contrary to the myth that homoeopathy is always slow, acute conditions (fever, cough, indigestion, allergic flares) often show noticeable improvement within hours. Long-standing chronic conditions (such as psoriasis, arthritis, or PCOD) improve progressively within 2 to 4 weeks as the root cause is resolved.

Can I take homoeopathic medicine alongside my existing allopathic prescriptions? ▼

Yes. Homoeopathic medicines do not chemically interact with or counteract allopathic medications like blood pressure, thyroid, or diabetes tablets. We simply advise maintaining a 20-30 minute interval between medications.
